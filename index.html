About the conference

Shaping the Next Paradigm of Intelligent, Autonomous, and Sustainable Computing

The International Conference on Next-Generation Computing and Innovations (ICNGCI 2027) is a premier two-day international forum hosted by the Sharda School of Computing Science and Engineering, Sharda University, Greater Noida, on 19–20 February 2027 in a hybrid format.

As computing continues to transform global industries and society, the future will not be defined by a single technology alone. Instead, it is emerging from the powerful convergence of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Blockchain, Next-Generation Networks & 6G, IoT, Robotics, and Quantum Computing. ICNGCI 2027 unites leading researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, policymakers, and innovators from across the globe to present cutting-edge breakthroughs and debate emerging directions.

Focusing on the transition from conventional computing toward intelligent, secure, resilient, and human-centric ecosystems, the conference welcomes original theoretical contributions, practical engineering solutions, industrial deployments, and interdisciplinary research addressing real-world challenges aligned with sustainable development goals.
